(1) 漸漸降低其 spatial resolution，何時會變得無法辨識?

x = imread('pikmin.png');
img = rgb2gray(x);
for i = 1:4
    subplot(2,2,i), imshow(imresize(imresize(img, 1/(2^(i+1))), 2^(i+1),'nearest'))
end
